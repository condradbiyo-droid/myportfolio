Condrad Lander Biyo


When I finish senior high and college, I want to be a seaman. It has been my dream for a long time. I’ve always admired those who work at sea, traveling the world while supporting their families. I know that being a seaman is not easy, but I am prepared for the challenges. I am ready to work hard and make sacrifices. One of the main reasons I want to become a seaman is because I want to give my family a good life. They have done so much for me, and I want to give back. My parents have worked hard just to provide for our needs. Sometimes, I see how tired they are, but they never complain. That motivates me to do my best in school.

I know education is the key to reaching my goals. Finishing senior high and college is my first step. I plan to study courses that will help me become qualified for a maritime career. I want to learn about navigation, ship safety, and marine engineering. I also want to improve my communication and leadership skills. Becoming a seaman will open many opportunities for me. I’ll be able to work with people from different countries and cultures. I’ll see new places and learn new things. But more than that, I’ll be able to earn a stable income. With that, I can support my family and give them a better future. I dream of building a house for my parents.

I want to send my siblings to school. I want to make sure they never have to worry about money again. I want to take care of them the way they’ve taken care of me. It’s not just about traveling or earning money—it’s about love and responsibility. Being a seaman is a way for me to turn my dreams into reality. I know it will take time, effort, and patience. But I am willing to do everything it takes. I believe in myself and my purpose. My family is my inspiration. Every day, I work hard in school because I know what I’m working for. One day, I’ll proudly wear my uniform and say, “I made it—for them.”
