Condrad Lander Biyo


Biyo lived in a peaceful village nestled between green hills, where life moved slowly and families stayed close. His father, a hardworking man, spent his days tending to their crops, ensuring the family had enough to live on. His mother, known for her gentle nature, ran a small bakery from their home, filling the house with the warm scent of bread and pastries. Biyo often helped them both, balancing school with his growing responsibilities at home.

Biyo’s little sister adored him and rarely left his side, always asking questions and trying to join in on whatever he was doing. Their grandmother was the heart of the home, always ready with a story from her youth or a tale filled with mystery and magic. Their grandfather, quiet but wise, spent his time carving wooden toys and teaching Biyo small lessons about life with every story or shared task. Despite their modest means, the family shared a deep bond and made the most of every moment together.

One year, a violent storm swept through the village, destroying crops and damaging homes, including their own. It was a difficult time, filled with uncertainty and hard choices, but the family never gave up. Together, they rebuilt their home and helped neighbors do the same. Through that hardship, Biyo learned that true strength comes not from wealth or power, but from love, unity, and the unwavering support of family.
